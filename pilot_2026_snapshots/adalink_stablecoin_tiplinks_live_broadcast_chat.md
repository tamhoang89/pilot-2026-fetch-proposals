### How will your product generate genuine usage - who transacts, why, and how often? Justify your previously declared targets as reasonable but ambitious enough to be considered valid.

The transactions come from **audience members sending stablecoin tips to creators**.

Users have two primary reasons to transact:

**Direct support:** financially supporting a creator whose work they value.

**Live interaction:** attaching a message to a tip that can become part of the creator's broadcast experience.

TipLinks are persistent and can remain across social profiles, video descriptions, livestreams, Discord communities, websites, newsletters, and other channels. Supporters may also tip the same creator across multiple broadcasts.

For planning purposes, 30 active creators averaging about 183 tips each during the measurement period would reach our target volume.

The target is ambitious relative to AdaLink's current transaction activity, but it is grounded in an existing mainnet product, an established creator ecosystem, proven creator-support behavior, paid creator distribution, and a reusable product designed for recurring use.

### What is your on-chain architecture, and why is it the right fit for selected integration(s) and this area of interest's technical requirements?

The proposed integration extends AdaLink’s existing Cardano mainnet architecture rather than introducing a separate payment system.

Today, AdaLink already supports CIP-30 wallet connection and signing, Cardano transaction construction, transaction confirmation, metadata, and wallet-to-wallet TipLinks.

Under this proposal, those capabilities will be extended so creators can choose which supported assets they accept, beginning with USDCx and other Cardano stablecoins. A supporter will open a TipLink, connect a wallet, select an accepted stablecoin, enter an amount and optional message, review the transaction, and sign it directly from their wallet. Funds will settle directly to the creator’s wallet, so AdaLink will not custody user assets.

After confirmation, AdaLink will index the transaction and mirror relevant data for tip history, notifications, moderation, and Live Broadcast Chat. The blockchain remains the source of truth for payment activity, while AdaLink controls the application-layer display of messages.

This architecture fits the Stablecoins integration because it enables direct on-chain stablecoin transfers, supports transaction identification by policy ID and metadata, minimizes custody and smart-contract risk, and builds on infrastructure already operating in AdaLink.

### What is your business model, and what keeps this running after the pilot? Who pays, and why does usage continue once grant funding ends?

AdaLink primarily operates on a **B2B2C model**, connecting businesses with creators and affiliates who help them reach and convert users. Creators and affiliates participate for free, while businesses pay through campaign fees, sponsored placements, and Affiliate Program Listings. We also charge transaction processing fees and may introduce fees for premium stickers or longer Live Broadcast Chat messages.

Stablecoin-enabled TipLinks strengthen the creator side without charging creators directly. Campaigns let creators earn from businesses for measurable results, while TipLinks let them earn directly from their audiences. Creator Profiles and performance data help them build a Web3 identity.

This creates a reinforcing model: better creator tools attract and retain creators, stronger participation improves campaign distribution, and better outcomes create more value for businesses and more opportunities for creators.

The grant funds the build and initial activation of this product.
