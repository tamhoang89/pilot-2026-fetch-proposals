### Who else solves this today - competitors/alternatives, and why does your approach win?

Users can assemble parts of the CIL workflow today, but across separate products: explorers provide blockchain visibility, wallets provide signing, DEXs provide liquidity, and market-data services provide pricing.

**CIL connects these capabilities through one intelligence layer.**

An explorer shows what a wallet holds; a DEX shows how to swap assets. CIL explains portfolio exposure, how a proposed action changes the portfolio, which stablecoin and liquidity options are relevant, and whether execution is reasonable before the user signs.

Unlike generic AI over blockchain APIs, CIL builds on CAP’s explicit Cardano semantic model and federated on-chain/off-chain reasoning. It complements existing infrastructure by turning fragmented data and services into informed, user-authorized actions.

### What is your on-chain architecture, and why is it the right fit for selected integration(s) and this area of interest's technical requirements?

**CIL uses an off-chain intelligence, on-chain execution architecture. CAP provides the existing Cardano data, Knowledge Graph and CIP-30 wallet primitives. CIL adds the new stablecoin action layer: it interprets a user objective, calculates portfolio implications, evaluates verified stablecoins and liquidity, and prepares the action for review.**

**The identified execution dependency is the Minswap Aggregator API. CIL will use Minswap for route estimation and construction of a valid unsigned swap transaction that can be signed by the user’s wallet. CIL will add the Pilot-required transaction tagging/footprint handling, validate the transaction against the user’s stated intent, and pass it to CAP’s existing CIP-30 signer. MOBR never receives private keys or user funds.**

**After signing and Cardano settlement, CIL tracks confirmation and re-indexes the resulting state into its intelligence layer. This keeps analysis off-chain while ensuring that qualifying activity is a genuine user-directed stablecoin transaction, not an artificial transaction created for Pilot metrics.**

**The primary third-party execution dependency is the Minswap Aggregator API, which CIL will use for stablecoin route estimation and construction of user-signable swap transactions. Stablecoin policies and CIP-30 wallets are additional infrastructure dependencies. Minswap is an integration provider, not represented as a project partner. No partner transaction volume is assumed in the adoption forecast.**

### What solution are you building, and what specific problem does it solve - for whom?

We are building the **Cardano Intelligence Layer (CIL)**, the next evolution of the Cardano Analytics Platform (CAP).

CAP established the foundation: a Cardano Knowledge Graph, natural-language analytics, federated on-chain/off-chain analysis, dashboards and an agentic query pipeline.

**CIL extends CAP from understanding Cardano to safely acting on Cardano through a new Stablecoin Action Center.**

Today, users must combine multiple tools to understand a wallet, value assets, assess stablecoin exposure, find liquidity and judge whether a proposed transaction is reasonable. CIL brings these steps into one intelligence-to-action workflow.

A user connects a wallet or analyzes a public address to understand composition, concentration, stablecoin exposure, market value and scenarios. They can then state an objective such as: “Reduce this wallet’s ADA allocation to 50% and hold the difference in Cardano stablecoins.”

CIL calculates the required amount, evaluates supported verified stablecoins and liquidity, obtains a non-custodial route, shows expected output, price impact and fees, and prepares the action for review. The user decides whether to proceed and signs with their own CIP-30 wallet.

CIL never holds funds or private keys or executes trades autonomously. The same workflow supports Cardano treasuries with monitoring, scenarios and human-approved actions.

**CAP helps users understand Cardano. CIL turns that intelligence into informed, user-authorized Cardano actions.**
