### What is your on-chain architecture, and why is it the right fit for selected integration(s) and this area of interest's technical requirements?

3riKE runs a dual chain design. Cardano holds all public facing assets: TricycleNFT is a PlutusV3 validator that mints and tracks CIP-25 tokens representing driver owned tricycles, and separate PlutusV3 contracts handle user registry, fractional investment, and the yield vault. Every share purchase and yield claim is a real on chain transaction with a public policy ID and script address, so ownership and payout history are auditable by anyone.

Midnight handles everything that needs to stay private: KYC commitments, the actual investment amounts per user, and yield balances, all proven with Compact ZK contracts so sensitive financial and identity data never touches a public ledger. Wallets are deterministic HD wallets derived from one master seed with HMAC-SHA256, so users never manage seed phrases or browser extensions.

This split is exactly what stablecoin settlement and programmable tokens need. Public asset ownership and compliance rules belong on Cardano where they can be verified and where CIP-0113 rules can enforce vesting and revenue sharing on the share tokens. Sensitive KYC and balance data belongs off the public chain, which Midnight already provides. Adding USDM or USDCx settlement means swapping the Paycrest testnet USDC leg for a Cardano native stablecoin at the same point in the flow, using infrastructure that already exists rather than building a new payment layer from scratch.

### What solution are you building, and what specific problem does it solve - for whom?

3riKE is building an AI-powered financial operating system for electric mobility, serving both mobility workers and everyday users seeking access to productive real-world assets. Drivers can access electric motorcycles and three-wheelers through structured ownership, while building financial identity through repayment and vehicle data. For users who cannot afford an entire vehicle, our fractionalization feature enables them to participate with smaller amounts, giving them access to tokenized real-world assets (RWAs) and potential income opportunities. This creates an alternative way to preserve and grow value amid currency devaluation, while directing capital into productive mobility infrastructure. By connecting drivers, fractional investors, lenders, and mobility operators, 3riKE creates a shared ecosystem where more people can participate in and benefit from the transition to electric mobility.

### Please provide details about the Technology Readiness Level selected for the integration you're proposing

The stablecoin piece of this proposal is not starting from zero. 3riKE already has a working fiat bridge through the Paycrest API that moves value between testnet USDC and real NGN, used today for driver repayments. That is a real, working proof of concept for stablecoin settlement inside our product.

What does not exist yet is settlement through a Cardano native stablecoin such as USDM or USDCx, and programmable compliance rules on the tricycle share tokens under CIP-0113. Those two pieces are experimental: we have the architecture worked out and the existing token and payment code to build on, but no testnet implementation of them yet. This grant funds building and testing that integration on Preprod before taking it to mainnet.
