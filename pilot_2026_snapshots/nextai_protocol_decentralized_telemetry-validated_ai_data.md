### What solution are you building, and what specific problem does it solve - for whom?

The Problem:

Up to 40% of public AI training data is corrupted by bot farms, synthetic spam, and low-quality filler. Simultaneously, enterprise B2B buyers lack reliable access to pristine, localized human data from emerging markets. Meanwhile, everyday contributors face complex Web3 onboarding, seed phrase management, and gas fee barriers that prevent broad financial inclusion.

Our Solution:

We are building the NEXT-AI Protocol on Cardano—a two-sided, telemetry-validated AI data engine that bridges global contributors with B2B enterprise.

1\. For AI Developers & Enterprise NEXT-AI delivers verified, bot-free datasets. Background telemetry scripts analyze micro-interaction dynamics  to score data purity before rewards are settled on-chain.

2\. For Everyday Contributors: Our Web Onboarding Portal provides zero-wallet friction. Users complete cognitive and environmental tasks, earning ADA/native rewards without managing complex gas fees. 

Who Benefits:

• Global AI developers acquiring high-fidelity training data

• Everyday contributors gaining borderless financial rewards.

• The Cardano ecosystem gaining sustained, high-volume real-world transaction utility.
