### What is your on-chain architecture, and why is it the right fit for selected integration(s) and this area of interest's technical requirements?

We propose a hybrid architecture that uses Cardano where decentralisation, ownership and verification create genuine value, while keeping high-volume learning data off-chain for privacy, performance and cost efficiency.

Each learner will be provisioned with or connected to a Cardano wallet. Olympus Academy and OLi AI Tutor will track learning activity off-chain, including course completion, Role Play progression, Zero to Hero pathways and XP.

When defined achievements are reached, the Cardano integration layer will create verifiable on-chain proofs/credentials linked to the learner’s wallet and self-sovereign digital identity. Sensitive learner data will not be written publicly on-chain; Cardano will anchor the proof and integrity of the achievement.

XP will be converted according to defined reward rules into $OLi, our Cardano native token, creating an on-chain reward pathway tied to genuine learning activity.

The architecture will use Cardano native assets, wallet infrastructure, transaction metadata and open standards/APIs, with the Catalyst-funded integration released open source.

This design fits education at scale: Cardano provides trust, portability, ownership and settlement, while OLi handles the data-intensive learning experience. It enables potentially millions of learning interactions without unnecessarily placing every event on-chain, while still generating measurable wallet, credential and token activity.

Education produces enormous value, yet learners rarely own their achievements, identity or the value created through learning. Credentials remain fragmented across institutions, learning data is locked inside platforms, and blockchain adoption still requires users to understand wallets, tokens and complex Web3 infrastructure before receiving any benefit.

Olympus Insights will solve both problems by integrating Cardano directly into our existing education ecosystem.

We will build an open-source Cardano integration layer connecting Olympus Academy and OLi AI Tutor to:

- Cardano wallets provisioned/linked for learners

- self-sovereign digital identity

The result is an education experience where learners can build a portable, verifiable record of skills and achievements while being progressively onboarded into Cardano.

Crucially, users will not need prior blockchain knowledge. They learn first; Cardano operates underneath the experience. As learners progress, they discover that their credentials, identity and rewards are truly theirs.

Rather than building blockchain infrastructure and then searching for adoption, we are bringing Cardano infrastructure to an established product ecosystem with 150,000+ existing learners.
