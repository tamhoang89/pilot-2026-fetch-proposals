### How will your product generate genuine usage - who transacts, why, and how often? Justify your previously declared targets as reasonable but ambitious enough to be considered valid.

We model 2 qualifying stablecoin transactions per user:

1. USDM/USDCx funding of WFit Credits;

2. USDM/USDCx withdrawal/settlement after Credits are used or earned through WChronicles, Battles, Raid rewards, subscriptions or other WFit services.

1,000 users × 2 = 2,000 transactions, with a 700 ADA fee target.

Post-launch pacing: Week 1: 100 external wallets, 150 stablecoin tx, 60 Credit purchases, 20 subscriptions and 30 withdrawals. Week 2: 150 additional wallets, 220 tx, 90 Credit purchases, 30 subscriptions and 50 withdrawals.

Remaining usage will be spread across recurring Credits funding, subscriptions and withdrawals, targeting roughly 250–300 tx per 5-day epoch. We will monitor per-epoch floors and ensure no single day contributes more than the 20% counted daily cap, avoiding launch day concentration.

### What solution are you building, and what specific problem does it solve - for whom?

**The problem**\
\
The problem is not simply moving stablecoins liquidity to Cardano. The problem is what happens after they arrive, users need a simple way to enter Cardano, use stablecoins for real-world utility, and exit without needing to understand bridges, swaps, ADA fees or DeFi.

WFit does not operate a new bridge. It provides the application and payment layer that abstracts existing interoperability infrastructure from the end user

The relation between USDC/USDT Deposit → Credits → Asset verification → Stablecoin settlement → Credit issuance → WFit utility → Redemption → USDM/USDCx withdrawal\
\
WFit turns stablecoins from an asset people hold into money they can actually use, users don't enter Cardano simply to hold USDM or USDCx. They enter Cardano to use stablecoins for fitness, subscriptions, games, sports, rewards and digital products.

**The product**

WFit will build a stablecoin payments and settlement layer into the new WFit ecosystem.

The complete user journey will be:\
\
→ WFit onboarding route

→ Exchange or other blockchain

(Ethereum – USDT, Solana – USDC, Abstract – USDCe/USDT, Injective – USDC, Robinhood – USDCe, Sui – USDC)

→ Connect Wallet or send manually from CEX

→ Credits — 1 USDC/USDT = 1 Credit

→ USDM or USDCx vault

→ WFit credit withdrawals in USDM or USDCx after usage in WFit ecosystem.

The product will include six core components.
