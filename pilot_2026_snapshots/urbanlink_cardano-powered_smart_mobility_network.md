### What is your on-chain architecture, and why is it the right fit for selected integration(s) and this area of interest's technical requirements?

UrbanLink will use Cardano as a trust and verification layer rather than placing the entire application on-chain. Vehicles, drivers and potentially riders will have blockchain-linked identities or verifiable credentials. Operational data such as routes, schedules and user information will remain off-chain, while relevant identity records and verification proofs are anchored on Cardano. This hybrid architecture minimizes on-chain data requirements while supporting privacy, scalability and efficient performance. It also allows UrbanLink to integrate Cardano into its existing mobility platform without rebuilding the entire application. The integration will focus on establishing verifiable identities for mobility participants and creating a trusted mechanism for confirming that vehicles, drivers and other participants are legitimate and authorized within the UrbanLink ecosystem.

### Who is your target market, and what evidence shows real demand/product-market fit?

UrbanLink’s primary market is daily commuters in Greater Accra who travel predictable routes to work and need reliable, scheduled shared transportation. Secondary customers include employers seeking better commuting options for staff, fleet operators and mobility partners.

Our demand is supported by research involving 1,029 respondents across employees of more than 25 companies in four major business centres. 97% preferred scheduled ride services, 87% expressed interest in scheduled bus transportation, and 91% were willing to pay more for greater predictability. ### What solution are you building, and what specific problem does it solve - for whom?

UrbanLink Mobility is a structured shared-mobility platform helping daily commuters access predictable and reliable transportation in Accra. For the Cardano Pilot, we are integrating an on-chain identity layer into our existing mobility product.

The problem is fragmented and centralized identity verification across shared mobility. Riders, drivers, vehicles, fleet operators and corporate partners need trusted identities and credentials, but current systems make verification dependent on individual platforms and repeated manual checks.

UrbanLink will use Cardano to create verifiable on-chain identities for riders, drivers and vehicles. Authorized parties will be able to verify relevant credentials and participation records without relying entirely on UrbanLink’s centralized database.

For riders, this provides a trusted identity that can support access to mobility services. For drivers and fleet operators, it enables verifiable identities and credentials for onboarding and eligibility checks. For UrbanLink and mobility partners, it creates a reusable trust layer that can support collaboration across operators and routes.

The Pilot will demonstrate this identity infrastructure in a real mobility environment, connecting Cardano’s on-chain identity capabilities to an existing product and real-world users.
