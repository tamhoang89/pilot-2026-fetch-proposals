### How will your product generate genuine usage - who transacts, why, and how often? Justify your previously declared targets as reasonable but ambitious enough to be considered valid.

Apex will generate genuine stablecoin usage by connecting businesses with users completing paid tasks, campaigns, and digital opportunities.

Businesses will fund campaigns and reward eligible participants, while users receive stablecoin payments for verified contributions. Students, developers, designers, and other skilled users can participate repeatedly, creating recurring transactions rather than one-time activity.

Our target of 1,000 stablecoin transactions is ambitious but achievable through university communities, Web3 communities, business campaigns, referrals, and early adopters. Multiple campaigns can generate repeated transactions as users complete verified activities.

We will measure transactions, active users, participating businesses, completed campaigns, rewards, and repeat usage. Apex will not create artificial, self-funded, circular, or unnecessary transactions to meet targets. Each transaction will represent genuine activity by a user or business.

### How will you reach and onboard real users - and what evidence backs your channels?

Apex will grow through universities, Web3 communities, developers, creators, referrals, and social media. Our first focus is universities, where we will partner with student technology, blockchain, entrepreneurship, and developer communities to create Apex campus communities and recruit early users and ambassadors. Students will discover Apex through demos, campaigns, community activities, and peer referrals.

We will also reach Web3 users through Telegram, X, developer networks, online communities, and partnerships with Web3 projects. Businesses running Apex campaigns can bring their existing communities onto the platform.

A referral system will support organic growth. Our existing Apex product, growing community, and ongoing university and Web3 outreach provide early evidence for these channels. During the pilot, we will track active users, completed activities, referrals, campaign participation, and retention, then scale the channels that deliver the strongest results.

### Who else solves this today - competitors/alternatives, and why does your approach win?

Existing alternatives include WURK, Galxe, and Zealy, which focus mainly on quests, microtasks, community engagement, or campaign distribution.

Apex combines these functions into one digital opportunity ecosystem. Users can find paid tasks and work, skilled professionals can connect with companies, businesses can hire talent and run campaigns, and Web3 projects can reach new users.

Our advantage is the combination of **talent discovery, paid opportunities, campaigns, engagement, reputation, and blockchain-based rewards** in one platform.

Apex is not just another task-to-earn app. It aims to help people **work, contribute, build reputation, and earn**, while giving businesses a single platform to find talent and acquire users.

### What is your on-chain architecture, and why is it the right fit for selected integration(s) and this area of interest's technical requirements?

The proposed Cardano stablecoin integration is at **TRL 3 (Experimental proof of concept)**. Apex has defined the architecture and use case for using Cardano stablecoins to support transparent campaign rewards and payments, but the integration has not yet been implemented or validated.

The Pilot will connect Apex campaign and reward workflows to Cardano wallet and stablecoin infrastructure, including wallet connectivity, payment and reward flows, transaction handling, and backend integration.

We will test the integration with selected users and businesses to validate secure and reliable reward settlement on Cardano. The goal is to progress from proof of concept to a tested, production-ready mainnet integration during the Pilot.

### What solution are you building, and what specific problem does it solve - for whom?

Apex is a blockchain-powered digital opportunity and engagement platform connecting students, skilled professionals, businesses, users, and Web3 projects in one ecosystem.

**The problem:** Students and skilled people struggle to find legitimate digital work, paid tasks, freelance opportunities, and practical experience. Businesses and Web3 projects struggle to find reliable talent, acquire genuine users, run campaigns, and measure engagement. These opportunities are fragmented across multiple platforms.

**Apex connects:**

- **Students:** Paid tasks, campaigns, communities, opportunities, and practical experience.

- **Developers, designers and professionals:** Profiles, portfolios, paid opportunities, and business connections.

- **Businesses and Web3 projects:** Talent discovery, hiring, campaigns, user acquisition, and engagement analytics.

- **Users and communities:** Opportunities, campaigns, reputation building, participation, and eligible rewards.

- **Cardano ecosystem:** A channel to reach students, developers, creators, businesses, and new users through real-world campaigns and opportunities.

Apex brings these groups together so people can **find opportunities, work, participate, build reputation, and earn**, while businesses can **find talent, acquire users, and grow**. Stablecoin integration will support transparent rewards and payments.
